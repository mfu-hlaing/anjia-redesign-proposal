Privacy policy

What this site stores, which is very little.

What this concept stores

This is a redesign concept. It has no server, no account system and no analytics. Nothing you type into a form on it is transmitted anywhere — the forms are demonstrations.

Three things are kept in your own browser, on your own device: your colour theme, your chosen display currency, and the residences you marked as saved. Clearing your browser data removes all three. None of it reaches us, because there is no us to reach.

What the live site does

anjia-home.net is operated by AnJia Property Management Co., Ltd and is not covered by this page. Its own privacy policy governs it.

A note we owe you

While measuring the live site for this proposal we found that its public interface returns owner names and contact numbers for resale listings to anyone who asks, without a login. None of that is shown on the site itself. We have not reproduced any of it here, and the dataset behind this concept was built with those fields excluded at source. We raised it with the firm as the first thing to fix, ahead of any design work.
