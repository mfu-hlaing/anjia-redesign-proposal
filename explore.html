    What you are looking at

    Real positions. Symbolic buildings. An approximate shore.

    Each of the 169 residences in the collection stands where its own recorded coordinates put it, so relative positions and distances between them are true. Its height follows the floor count in the record, exaggerated so a tower still reads from across the bay; the footprint and the shape are symbolic. An amber roof means the residence is currently offered with a foreign-ownership route — freehold, or a unit inside the building's foreign quota.

    The shoreline was drawn by hand and fitted to where the beachfront residences actually stand. It is there for orientation. There is no street, no plot boundary and no view line, because none of those were measured. Where a resale record names its floor, that floor is lit.

    The light is the light in Pattaya now, unless you choose another hour. Nothing in the collection changes with the hour — only the sky.

    Said plainly

    Where the record and the map disagree

    A few records carry coordinates that cannot be where the residence is. They are listed here rather than quietly placed somewhere plausible, and the desk confirms the location on request.
