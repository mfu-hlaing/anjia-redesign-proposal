Pattaya Events & Festivals 2026: What to Look Forward to from July Onward

The second half of 2026 is set to be one of the busiest and most exciting periods for Pattaya, with a packed calendar of sporting events, film festivals, international fireworks displays, tennis championships, water sports competitions, wellness experiences, and world-class music festivals. These events will further strengthen Pattaya's reputation as one of Thailand's premier destinations, welcoming visitors from around the globe throughout the remainder of the year.

Whether you're visiting to participate in a marathon, enjoy a family vacation, explore cultural attractions, or attend an international music festival, here are some of the top events in Pattaya you won't want to miss from July 2026 onward.

July 2026: Amazing Thailand Pattaya Marathon

18–19 July 2026 | Pattaya City

One of Pattaya's signature sporting events, the Amazing Thailand Pattaya Marathon 2026 attracts thousands of runners from Thailand and around the world. The event features multiple race categories suitable for professional athletes, amateur runners, and fitness enthusiasts alike.

Known for its scenic coastal route, breathtaking sea views, and vibrant international atmosphere, the marathon offers much more than just a race—it delivers an unforgettable travel experience in one of Thailand's most popular beach destinations.

Best for: Runners, sports enthusiasts, health-conscious travelers, and families.

August 2026: Pattaya Film Festival

August 2026 | Pattaya City

For lovers of arts and culture, the Pattaya Film Festival showcases the city's growing reputation as a destination for cinema and creative tourism.

The festival is expected to feature film screenings, filmmaker discussions, and cultural activities hosted throughout Pattaya, offering visitors a unique experience beyond the city's famous beaches and nightlife.

Best for: Film lovers, art enthusiasts, couples, and cultural travelers.

October 2026: International Bikini Beach Race

31 October 2026 | Pattaya Beach

The International Bikini Beach Race combines fitness, fashion, music, and a lively beach party atmosphere into one of Pattaya's most colorful annual events.

Popular among fun runners and groups of friends, the race offers an energetic outdoor experience filled with entertainment and seaside excitement.

Best for: Fun runners, groups of friends, and beach lovers.

October 2026: Family Halloween Celebrations

Late October | Hotels and Attractions Across Pattaya

During Halloween, many hotels, resorts, shopping centers, and tourist attractions across Pattaya host family-friendly celebrations featuring costume parties, creative workshops, themed dinners, and activities for children.

Best for: Families, children, and holiday travelers.

November 2026: Pattaya International Fireworks Festival

27–28 November 2026 | Pattaya Beach

The Pattaya International Fireworks Festival is one of the city's largest annual events, featuring spectacular fireworks displays from participating countries over two unforgettable evenings.

Combined with live entertainment, music, and the beautiful beachfront setting, the festival attracts visitors of all ages.

Best for: Couples, families, photographers, and first-time visitors.

November 2026: MT700 ITF Fitz Club Masters Championship

November 2026 | Fitz Club, Royal Cliff Hotels Group

This prestigious international tennis tournament welcomes experienced players from around the world to compete at the renowned Fitz Club.

The championship reflects Pattaya's growing appeal as a destination for premium sports tourism and healthy lifestyles.

Best for: Tennis enthusiasts, wellness travelers, and visitors staying in the Pratumnak area.

December 2026: WGP#1 Waterjet World Cup

9–13 December 2026 | Jomtien Beach

The WGP#1 Waterjet World Cup brings together the world's top jet ski racers for an exciting international competition along Jomtien Beach.

Spectators can enjoy thrilling races while experiencing Pattaya's vibrant seaside atmosphere.

Best for: Water sports fans, families, and adventure travelers.

December 2026: Wonderfruit Festival

3–7 December 2026 | The Fields at Siam Country Club

The internationally acclaimed Wonderfruit Festival returns with an inspiring mix of live music, art installations, wellness experiences, sustainable living, culinary events, and cultural programs.

Recognized as one of Thailand's most unique festivals, Wonderfruit celebrates creativity, community, and environmental sustainability.

Best for: Music lovers, artists, wellness enthusiasts, and lifestyle travelers.

December 2026: Tomorrowland Thailand

11–13 December 2026 | Wisdom Valley, Chonburi

Tomorrowland Thailand is expected to make its debut in Chonburi, near Pattaya, bringing one of the world's most iconic electronic dance music festivals to Thailand.

The event is anticipated to attract thousands of international visitors while further enhancing Pattaya and Chonburi as premier destinations for global entertainment and music tourism.

Best for: EDM fans, international travelers, and groups of friends.

Late December 2026: Pattaya Countdown

Late December | Pattaya Beach

Pattaya's annual Countdown Festival is among the city's most anticipated celebrations, featuring live concerts, entertainment, food vendors, spectacular fireworks, and a festive beachfront atmosphere as visitors welcome the New Year together.

Hotels, restaurants, and attractions throughout the city experience their busiest period during this holiday season.

Best for: New Year holidaymakers, families, couples, and groups of friends.

Conclusion

From July onward, Pattaya offers an impressive lineup of sporting events, cultural festivals, international competitions, music festivals, and year-end celebrations, making it one of Thailand's most vibrant destinations throughout the second half of 2026.

Visitors planning a trip during this period are encouraged to book accommodations, transportation, and restaurants well in advance—especially during major events such as the Pattaya Marathon, Pattaya International Fireworks Festival, Wonderfruit Festival, Tomorrowland Thailand, and the Pattaya Countdown , when demand for hotels and holiday accommodations is at its highest.
