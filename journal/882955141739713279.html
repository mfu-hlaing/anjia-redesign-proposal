Market  ·  2026-08-26

Thai real estate is attractive to foreign investors, with rental yields in Pattaya and Phuket highlighted at 8-10%.

TRECA says slowing domestic demand is prompting real estate agencies to target international buyers, highlighting Bangkok, Phuket, Pattaya, and Krabi as prime investment destinations.

Thailand's real estate market is drawing growing interest from overseas investors as strong rental returns and an attractive lifestyle continue to outweigh domestic economic challenges. With rental yields reaching 8–10% in Pattaya and Phuket , these destinations are becoming increasingly appealing to investors seeking long-term income and capital appreciation.

Domestic Slowdown Drives Focus on International Buyers

Thailand's property sector is facing multiple headwinds, including slower economic growth, elevated household debt, and stricter mortgage lending policies. As domestic purchasing power weakens, developers and real estate agencies are shifting their focus toward international buyers, who continue to see Thailand as an attractive destination for property investment.

According to Ms. Darunee Rungruangphon , Secretary-General of the Thai Real Estate Broker Association (TRECA) and Managing Director of Darvid Property Service Co., Ltd. , the current economic environment has encouraged real estate professionals to expand their presence in overseas markets.

Foreign buyers remain interested in Thailand not only as a place to live but also as a destination for investment and rental income. Key markets include Bangkok, Phuket, Pattaya, and Krabi , where tourism, infrastructure, and long-term residential demand continue to support the property sector.

Ultra-Luxury Projects Raise Thailand's Global Profile

Another notable trend is the growing number of ultra-luxury residential developments launched by leading developers across Thailand's prime locations.

This reflects a shift in the country's real estate market, which is no longer competing solely on affordability but is increasingly attracting high-net-worth individuals and global investors seeking premium residential assets.

Bangkok continues to serve as Thailand's commercial and financial center, while Phuket and Pattaya benefit from strong tourism demand and long-stay visitors. Krabi is also emerging as a promising destination thanks to its natural attractions and expanding tourism industry.

For many overseas buyers, purchasing a condominium or house in Thailand is no longer simply about owning a holiday home. Property has become an investment asset capable of generating rental income while providing a lifestyle base for extended stays in the country.

Thailand's Competitive Advantages Extend Beyond Property Prices

Thailand's appeal to foreign buyers goes well beyond affordable real estate.

The country offers an attractive combination of affordable living costs, high quality of life, internationally recognized healthcare services, beautiful natural attractions, renowned hospitality, world-famous cuisine, and an expanding network of international schools.

These factors make Thailand particularly attractive for expatriate families and long-term residents. As international travel continues to recover, increasing tourism demand is also driving growth in the long-term rental market, further supporting residential property sales.

Pattaya and Phuket Lead with Strong Rental Yields

One of the strongest drivers of foreign investment remains Thailand's competitive Rental Yield .

According to industry data, Pattaya delivers average rental yields of approximately 8–9%, while Phuket achieves around 8–10% . These figures compare favorably with many major international cities, where residential rental yields typically range between 2–5% .

The relatively high returns make Thai real estate particularly attractive to investors seeking consistent rental income in addition to long-term capital appreciation.

As a result, agencies specializing in international buyers continue to see strong growth opportunities despite softer domestic demand.

A New Strategy: Identify Demand Before Finding Supply

Mr. Kasinon Nonthirarangsi , Managing Director of Skylook Property 88 Co., Ltd. and a specialist in luxury and international property transactions, believes many Thai real estate agents are interested in expanding into overseas markets but often face challenges related to language and international transaction procedures.

However, he emphasizes that language should not be viewed as the primary barrier.

International buyers value expertise, credibility, professionalism, and transaction security far more than perfect language skills.

Instead of listing properties first and then searching for buyers, agents should adopt a "Demand Before Supply" strategy—understanding what investors are looking for before sourcing suitable properties.

This customer-focused approach reduces inventory risk while increasing the likelihood of successful transactions.

Understanding Buyers Is the Key to Success

Foreign buyers are not a single homogeneous market.

Different customer groups—including expatriates married to Thai nationals, investors from neighboring countries, retirees, and buyers seeking rental investments—have unique objectives, budgets, and lifestyle preferences.

Understanding these differences enables agents to recommend the right locations, property types, layouts, and interior designs that best match each client's needs.

As the role of real estate professionals evolves, agents are becoming investment advisors rather than simply property brokers.

Looking Ahead

While Thailand's domestic property market continues to face economic challenges, international buyers are providing an important source of demand for the sector.

Success in today's global market requires far more than English communication skills or overseas marketing campaigns. It depends on understanding clients, identifying investment opportunities, and providing professional guidance throughout the purchasing process.

In an increasingly competitive environment, the agencies that thrive will be those that understand international investors, anticipate market demand, and deliver confidence at every stage of the property investment journey.
