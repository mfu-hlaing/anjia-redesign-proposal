The residence

Atlantis Condo Resort Jomtien is a large resort-style condominium developed by Blue Sky Development Co., Ltd., located on Jomtien Second Road, approximately 700 metres from Jomtien Beach. Launched in 2012 and completed in 2015, the development comprises five 8-storey residential buildings with approximately 1,100 units on a 17,600-square-metre site. Inspired by a tropical resort concept, the project features a spectacular lagoon-style swimming pool, water park with slides, waterfalls, landscaped gardens, fitness centre, sauna, and family-oriented facilities. Its attractive location and comprehensive amenities make it a popular choice for permanent living, holiday homes, and rental investment.

The developer

Blue Sky Development Co., Ltd. (Blue Sky Group) — Blue Sky Development Co., Ltd. (Blue Sky Group) is a Pattaya-based real estate developer specializing in resort-style residential condominiums. The company is recognized for creating developments that combine tropical landscaping, extensive recreational facilities, and practical residential layouts. Atlantis Condo Resort Jomtien is one of its signature projects, offering a distinctive water-park concept, family-friendly amenities, and strong appeal for both homeowners and property investors.

Getting there

Atlantis Condo Resort Jomtien is located on Jomtien Second Road, close to Wat Boon Junction. The development is approximately 500–700 metres from Jomtien Beach, with convenient access to Sukhumvit Road, Thepprasit Road, and Central Pattaya, making it ideal for both residents and holiday visitors.
